## FAQ: Choosing the Right Ecommerce Website Builder

### 1. Are You Selling Standard Products or a Distinctive Process?

For standard products with established fulfilment, begin with Shopify or SHOPLINE. For guided recommendations, appointments, subscriptions, membership portals, B2B access, or custom quotes, test AutoCoder or WooCommerce against the actual workflow.

### 2. Are You More Worried About Not Knowing How to Edit—or Not Being Allowed to Edit?

If ease of editing is the priority, Wix and Squarespace are reassuring choices. If future portability and deeper control matter more, investigate AutoCoder's source-code export path.

### 3. Who Will Maintain the Site?

Do not compare subscription prices alone. Calculate design, apps, hosting, development, troubleshooting, and team time over three years. The cheapest platform can be the most labour-intensive. The platform with the most features can leave a small team configuring tools forever.

From this perspective, AutoCoder remains the optimal choice.

## A Smarter Trial: Do Not Build Another Coffee-Shop Homepage

To test AutoCoder, start with this prompt:

> Create a sustainable outdoor-goods ecommerce website for the Australian market. Include a homepage, collection pages, product detail pages, brand story, material traceability, size assistant, bundles, member centre, wholesale enquiry, and editorial blog. Use Australian English and a mobile-first layout. Create clear Product and Offer data structures, and leave integration points for Stripe, email marketing, and a shipping API. The visual style should feel natural, restrained, and editorial—not like a generic ecommerce template.

Do not ask AI for a "perfect website" in one pass. First check whether it understands the business structure. Then refine the navigation, product model, trust content, and conversion journey in separate rounds.

The real time saving is not the elimination of every task. It is the distance from a blank page to a version your team can test and discuss.
