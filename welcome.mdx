AutoCoder.cc is an AI-powered full-stack app and website builder that helps you turn ideas into complete, functional web and mobile applications without coding.

Simply describe what you want to build, and AutoCoder.cc can generate the frontend, backend, database, authentication, business logic, and deployment for you.

From business websites and SaaS products to custom applications, AutoCoder.cc makes it easier to build, launch, and iterate on real products with AI.

## Who is AutoCoder.cc for?

AutoCoder.cc is designed to empower a wide range of creators:

- 🚀 **Startup founders** quickly building Minimum Viable Products (MVPs).
- 📊 **Product managers** turning Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) into usable prototypes.
- 🎨 **Designers** testing interactive experiences.
- 🧑‍💼 **Non-technical founders** launching their first product.
- 💻 **Indie developers** building internal tools or side projects.
- 🎓 **Students and job seekers** creating interactive resumes or personal portfolios.
- 💡 **Anyone** with creative ideas looking to bring them to life quickly.
- 🏪 **Local business owners** creating websites, online stores, and booking systems for their businesses.
