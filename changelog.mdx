<Update label="Jul 25, 2026" description="Payments">
### Cryptocurrency payments now available

AutoCoder.cc now supports cryptocurrency payment flows for generated websites and applications. Projects can integrate multi-asset checkout with **USDT, USDC, BTC, and ETH**, support on-chain transaction verification and confirmation tracking, and automatically trigger business actions such as content access, membership activation, bookings, and order fulfillment after successful payment. The update also adds transaction management capabilities, including payment status tracking, transaction records, and operational visibility from the admin side. Read the full update in [Cryptocurrency Payments Now Available](/blog/crypto-payments).
</Update>
