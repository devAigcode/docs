## Turn a Google Maps Business Listing into a Website

Transform a Google Maps business listing into a professional, editable website — without writing code or manually organizing business information.

Simply paste the business's Google Maps Share link. AutoCoder will retrieve the available public information, display it for confirmation, and generate a complete website based on the business listing.

---

## Core Features

- **Google Maps Data Import**: import available business details directly from a Google Maps Share link.
- **Business Information Preview**: review the imported information before generating the website to ensure the correct business has been selected.
- **One-Click Website Generation**: click **Generate Website** to create an editable, full-stack business website.
- **Automatic Content Creation**: turn existing business information, images, and customer reviews into structured website content.
- **No Coding Required**: create and customize a professional business website without coding or web design experience.

---

## Information That May Be Imported

Depending on what is publicly available on Google Maps, the preview may include:

- Business name and categories
- Address and phone number
- Rating and review count
- Business website
- Opening hours
- Business photos
- Customer reviews

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Google Maps Website Generator">
    After logging in, click **Paste a Google Maps link to create a full-stack website** above the input box on the AutoCoder homepage, or use the direct-access link at the end of this page.

    <Frame caption="Google Maps Generator entry above the prompt box on the AutoCoder homepage.">
      ![AutoCoder homepage with the Paste a Google Maps link to create a full-stack website button highlighted above the prompt input box](/images/google-maps-generator/01.png "Google Maps Website Generator entry on the AutoCoder homepage")
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="The Google Maps Website Generator page, ready for a business Share link.">
      ![Google Maps Website Generator page titled Generate a Website from Your Google Maps Reviews, with an arrow pointing to the link input field](/images/google-maps-generator/02.png "Google Maps Website Generator link input")
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the business on Google Maps">
    Open the specific business listing you want to build a website for.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Share and copy the business link">
    On the listing, select **Share**, then **Copy link**.

    <Frame caption="Select Share on the Google Maps business listing.">
      ![Google Maps listing for Hard Rock Cafe with the Share button highlighted in the action row](/images/google-maps-generator/03.png "Share button on a Google Maps business listing")
    </Frame>

    <Frame caption="Select Copy link in the Share dialog.">
      ![Google Maps Share dialog for Hard Rock Cafe with the Copy Link button highlighted](/images/google-maps-generator/04.png "Copy link in the Google Maps Share dialog")
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste the link into the AutoCoder Google Maps Website Generator">
    Paste the copied Share link into the input field on the generator page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose a language">
    Select the language for the generated website from the dropdown.

    <Frame caption="Choose the website language before generating.">
      ![Choose a language dropdown on the Google Maps Website Generator listing options such as English, español, français, and Deutsch](/images/google-maps-generator/05.png "Language selection in the Google Maps Website Generator")
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the imported business information">
    Confirm that the preview shows the correct business, with its details, photos, opening hours, and customer reviews.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Generate Website to create the project">
    Select **Generate Website** to create the editable, full-stack project.

    <Frame caption="Review the imported business details, then select Generate Website.">
      ![What We Got preview panel for a KFC listing showing address, rating, categories, phone number, photos, opening hours, and customer reviews, with the Generate Website button highlighted](/images/google-maps-generator/06.png "Business preview and Generate Website button")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Please use the Share link from a specific business listing rather than a search-results page, directions link, or the general Google Maps homepage.
</Warning>
