Website ideas rarely start as text. They start as a photo, a screenshot, a logo, a design reference, a document full of content, or a thought you would rather just say out loud. AutoCoder.cc makes website creation and updates more intuitive with multimodal AI: images, documents, and voice instructions are transformed into real website changes, all within one seamless workflow.
