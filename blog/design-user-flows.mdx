## Why designing user flows matters

A product is more than a collection of screens. When pages are generated in isolation, the connections between them are left for someone to fill in later: the data that carries over, the states that change, the rules that decide what a user can do next. Designing around the journey means screens, interactions, data states, and business logic are connected from the start.

By designing around what users need to accomplish rather than simply listing pages, you can build complete applications with clear logic, connected features, and editable user flows. Even without product design or coding experience, you can map a full journey and refine it as you go.
