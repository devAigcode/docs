Build your first website or application with AutoCoder.cc, starting with a simple idea and turning it into a working project.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to AutoCoder.cc">
    Register and log in to your AutoCoder.cc account. You can use Google login for quick access. Once logged in, you can:

    - **Create** new projects
    - **Edit** generated applications
    - **Browse** community projects
    - **Manage** projects in the **Workspace**

    <Frame>
      ![Log in to AutoCoder.cc](/images/getting-started-login.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your idea">
    After logging in, you will enter the AutoCoder.cc homepage. In the input box, describe the application you want to create in three ways:

    **Enter a Prompt**

    Describe what you want to build in natural language. For example:

    - "Create a restaurant website"
    - "Build a landing page for a product"
    - "Create a task management app"

    **Enter a Keyword**

    Enter a keyword such as "restaurant" to automatically match relevant templates and quickly get started with your project.

    <Frame>
      ![Keyword matching for project templates](/images/getting-started-keyword.png)
    </Frame>

    **Start with a Template**

    Choose a template from SparkVibe or Templates and remix it to fit your needs.

    <Frame>
      ![SparkVibe and Templates in the sidebar](/images/getting-started-templates.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![Remix a SparkVibe project](/images/getting-started-sparkvibe.png)
    </Frame>

    Choose the approach that works best for you, then continue building and refining your project with AutoCoder.cc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate your first project">
    Click **Generate**, and AutoCoder.cc will automatically build your application through Progressive Build — from Draft to Requirements to Full Project.

    Your requirements can come from text, images, files, wireframes, flowcharts, or voice. AutoCoder.cc understands these multimodal inputs and quickly generates an editable Draft of your application.

    Once you see the Draft, you can preview and edit it.

    <Frame>
      ![Editable Draft stage](/images/getting-started-draft.png)
    </Frame>

    AutoCoder.cc also organizes your inputs into a structured Requirement List, which helps guide the generation of the Full Project.

    <Frame>
      ![Generate Requirement List](/images/getting-started-generate-requirements.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![Structured Requirement List](/images/getting-started-requirement-list.png)
    </Frame>

    The build typically includes:

    - **Draft**: UI style and page layout
    - **Requirements**: Pages, user flows, features, and logic
    - **Full Project**: Frontend, backend, database, and functional logic

    You can edit your project at every stage, giving you more control over the final result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refine your project">
    After generating the full project, continue improving your application with multimodal AI chat. You can:

    - **Add** new pages
    - **Modify** page structures
    - **Update** functional logic
    - **Optimize** content and layouts

    AutoCoder.cc will update your project based on your instructions, helping you continuously refine and improve your application.

    <Frame>
      ![Refine the full project with AI chat](/images/getting-started-refine.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Website Publishing and Domain Setup">
    Once your website is generated, you can publish it directly to the web with flexible domain options:

    - **Free Subdomain**: Get a public Website URL and secure Backend URL instantly.
    - **Purchase a Domain**: Search for and purchase a new domain.
    - **Custom Domain**: Connect your own domain with CNAME and SSL support.
    - **Publishing Settings**: Choose whether to hide the platform badge.
    - **One-Click Publish**: Verify your settings and click Publish to launch your website.

    <Frame>
      ![Website publishing and domain setup](/images/getting-started-publish.png)
    </Frame>

    These functions are introduced in detail in the **Features** section.
  </Step>
</Steps>
