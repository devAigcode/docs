<Update label="Sep 11, 2026" description="Platform">
### Orders & payments system upgrade

AutoCoder.cc has upgraded its order and payment systems and launched a brand-new **Order Management** feature. Users can now easily view their order status and order history, making it simpler to manage and track purchases.

We've also fixed several known issues in the payment process, further improving the stability and overall experience of purchasing, subscribing, and making payments.

### Product & comparison pages are now live

We've launched a new **Competitive Comparison** page, making it easier to compare AutoCoder.cc with similar products across features, use cases, and user experience. Get a clear overview of what each product offers, quickly understand the key differences, and find the solution that's the right fit for your project.

### Expanded multilingual website building

AutoCoder.cc continues to expand its multilingual capabilities. Google Maps business website generation now supports **Arabic** and **Russian**. Users can simply paste a business link, with no keywords required, to quickly generate a website in the corresponding language, delivering a more localized website-building and user experience for customers in different regions.
</Update>

<Update label="Aug 18, 2026" description="Feature">
### Multimodal editing now live

AutoCoder.cc now supports multimodal inputs, including **images, documents, and voice**, enabling a more natural and flexible way to generate and modify websites.

Users can upload screenshots as visual references or provide website screenshots for AutoCoder.cc to analyze and recreate the page content and visual design. They can also upload data documents for the system to reference and incorporate during website creation, or use voice input to describe website generation or modification requests directly. Read more in [Build with Images, Files, and Voice: Multimodal AI](/blog/multimodal-ai-editing).
</Update>

<Update label="Jul 25, 2026" description="Payments & pricing">
### Cryptocurrency payments now available

AutoCoder.cc now supports cryptocurrency payment flows for generated websites and applications. Projects can integrate multi-asset checkout with **USDT, USDC, BTC, and ETH**, support on-chain transaction verification and confirmation tracking, and automatically trigger business actions such as content access, membership activation, bookings, and order fulfillment after successful payment. The update also adds transaction management capabilities, including payment status tracking, transaction records, and operational visibility from the admin side. Read the full update in [Cryptocurrency Payments Now Available](/blog/crypto-payments).

### Subscription plans and pricing upgrade

AutoCoder.cc has launched a new four-tier pricing structure: **Free, Starter, Standard, and Pro**. All paid plans include monthly credits (30 / 80 / 220).

A new Starter plan is now available for new users. New users can enjoy a limited-time 20% discount on Starter, Standard, and Pro within 48 hours of registration. After the first month, Starter automatically renews and upgrades to Standard.

Paid plans unlock more capabilities, including custom domain binding, complete multi-page projects with data and admin dashboards, login and payment API compatibility, dedicated support, instant multi-role switching, and project downloads. Standard also includes 10GB of storage and X10 Boost, while Pro offers unlimited custom domains and subdomains, unlimited multi-page full-stack projects, access to advanced features, domain purchasing, exclusive top-up discounts, and priority email support.

This update also introduces annual billing, with 2 months free when paying annually. Users can also earn free credits by completing tasks or referring friends.
</Update>

<Update label="Jul 13, 2026" description="Pricing">
### Additional credit packages

AutoCoder.cc now supports one-time credit package purchases. If the credits included in your current plan are not enough, you can purchase additional credits without upgrading or changing your existing plan.

Credit packages are one-time purchases and do not auto-renew. Your existing subscription will continue to renew according to its original monthly or annual billing cycle.
</Update>

<Update label="Jun 7, 2026" description="Feature">
### Multilingual project generation

AutoCoder.cc now supports multilingual project generation. When creating a project, simply specify your preferred language in the prompt, and AutoCoder will generate the project content in that language. When creating a website from a Google Maps business link, you can also select your preferred language before generation, making multilingual and localized website creation easier.

### Google Maps business link website generator

AutoCoder.cc now lets you generate a complete website from a Google Maps business link. Simply find the business on Google Maps, copy its share link, and paste it into the [AutoCoder Google Maps Generator](https://www.autocoder.cc/en/google-maps-generator). AI will read the business's publicly available information, such as name, address, contact details, opening hours, ratings, and photos, and turn it into a professional website with customer-facing pages, online booking, and an admin dashboard.

No need to organize everything from scratch, and no coding or web design experience required. See it in action in [From a Google Maps Link to a Caesar Sauce Website](/blog/google-maps-link-to-website).
</Update>

<Update label="Apr 8, 2026" description="Backend">
### Backend capability upgrade: multiple roles and complex workflows

AutoCoder.cc has further enhanced its backend generation capabilities, now supporting **multi-role architectures**, role-based permission management, and more complex business logic. Users can describe their requirements to generate different roles, such as administrators, staff, and customers, within a single application, with dedicated pages, data-access scopes, and permissions for each role.

For example, in an appointment management system, administrators can manage all appointments and personnel; service staff can view and process appointments assigned to them; and customers can submit and check their own appointment information.

This capability is well suited to role-based collaboration scenarios, including appointment services, e-commerce management, education platforms, and internal business management systems.
</Update>

<Update label="Jun 24, 2025" description="Feature">
### Website builder controller launch

The first version of the AutoCoder.cc Controller is now officially live, establishing a foundational workflow from requirement input and structured confirmation to website generation, result preview, and ongoing revisions.

Users can create projects by describing their needs in text or uploading files. AI automatically organizes requirements such as the project's purpose, target users, visual style, and feature modules, which can be further refined through manual editing or multi-turn conversations before generation.

Once a website has been generated, users can review the pages and code, then use chat to add or remove pages, adjust the overall style, or edit specific pages and components. The Controller also supports project history, version status, team member management, and project permission management.
</Update>
