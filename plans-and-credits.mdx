## Free plan & upgrades

### Core capabilities and limits of the Free plan

The Free plan allows you to experience vibe coding at zero cost. You get access to backend project support, **1GB disk space**, unlimited private projects, and static single-page view-only demos. You start with **6 credits**, and you can earn extra credits by completing missions or sharing with friends on the **Plans & credit usage** page.

_Note: The Free plan does not support source-code download/export, custom domain binding, multi-page full-stack projects, priority customer service, or early access to model upgrades and new product features._

### The core value of upgrading to a paid plan

Upgrading to **Starter**, **Standard** or **Pro** truly unlocks AutoCoder.cc's potential as your "AI development team." You get higher monthly credit allocations to build and iterate on projects, unlock project download / source-code export capability, custom domain functionality, expanded disk storage, full multi-page full-stack project support, and dedicated support tiers. Pro-tier users gain unlimited custom domains, priority email support and exclusive access to premium platform features.
