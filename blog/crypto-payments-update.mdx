*Build the complete journey from crypto checkout and on-chain verification to automatic fulfillment and transaction operations.*

Today, we're introducing cryptocurrency payment support for websites and applications generated with [AutoCoder.cc](https://www.autocoder.cc/platform?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=latest&utm_campaign=CryptoPayments "Build full-stack apps with AutoCoder.cc"). Builders can now create payment experiences that do more than display a wallet address: they can guide a customer through checkout, verify the transaction on-chain, fulfill the purchase, and surface the result to an operations team.

## From checkout to business outcome

Crypto payments often become disconnected from the rest of an application. A customer sends funds, but the product still needs a reliable way to recognize the order, monitor confirmations, grant access, and preserve a record for support. The new payment capability connects those steps into one application workflow.

A generated experience can present the purchase total, available assets, compatible networks, payment QR code, receiving address, expiry window, and live verification status. When the configured confirmation depth is reached, the application can complete the order and continue to the next business action automatically.

## One checkout, the information customers need

The checkout brings the order summary and payment controls into one view. Customers can select a displayed asset and settlement network, scan a QR code or copy the receiving address, and follow the order's confirmation progress without switching between separate purchase pages.

## Verified payment can fulfill the purchase automatically

After the transaction reaches the required on-chain confirmation depth, the order moves from Pending to Successful. CryptoEdu then unlocks the purchased course automatically, demonstrating how payment status can become a trigger for real application logic.

<Frame caption="Figure 3 | On-chain confirmation completes the order and unlocks enrollment">
  ![Payment confirmation screen showing the notification "Payment verified and course enrollment unlocked", a 100% finalized block progress bar, 249 USDT paid on USDT TRC20, and the settlement time](/images/blog/crypto-payments-update/03.png "Automatic course enrollment after on-chain payment confirmation")
</Frame>

The same pattern can support other outcomes: delivering a digital product, activating a membership, confirming a reservation, or starting an e-commerce fulfillment process. The business action changes, but the underlying flow remains consistent: create the order, receive payment, verify the transaction, and complete the customer journey.

## Built-in visibility for operations

Customer checkout is only one side of a payment system. The generated admin experience can give teams visibility into total revenue, order volume, payment success rate, network distribution, and live customer activity. Individual records can be searched or filtered by status for support and operational follow-up.
